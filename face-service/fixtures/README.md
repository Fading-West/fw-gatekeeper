# Synthetic faces

These six fictional adults were generated with the built-in imagegen tool on
2026-10-09, then split from a 1536×1024 contact sheet into 512×512 JPEGs
(quality 92). No photographs of real people were downloaded or used.

Prompt: “Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: synthetic face detector
regression fixture. Generate one photorealistic contact sheet, exactly 3 columns
by 2 rows of six entirely fictional adult head-and-shoulder portraits, no
resemblance to actual people or celebrities. Each tile is square and has one
centered face, frontal or slight 10 degree turn, entire head visible, plain pale
gray background, shoulders at bottom, normal soft indoor lighting. Diverse ages
25-60, skin tones, male and female, some glasses, beard, short and long hair.
Faces should take up about half the width of each tile. Equal tile dimensions,
no gutters, no borders, no text, no watermark. This sheet will be split into six
test images for comparing face detectors, so use realistic facial features and
no other faces.”

These fixtures verify implementation parity. They cannot establish field
recognition accuracy, fairness, or robustness under factory lighting or pose.
