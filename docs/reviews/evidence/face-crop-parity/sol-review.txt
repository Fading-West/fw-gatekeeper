Reproduced and fixed:
- Chunked/unknown-length and idle oversized requests could exceed admission's
  declared-byte budget. Enforce received-byte limits and concurrent accounting.
- FastAPI parsed unauthenticated JSON before endpoint authentication. Reject
  invalid credentials in admission before buffering/parsing.
- A valid 32,364,030-byte /match request with 15,500 512-d vectors returned 200
  and peaked at 703,308 KiB (686.8 MiB), above the 512 MB plan. Limit /match to
  8 MiB and charge four times body bytes against the 32 MiB shared budget.
- /match retained its decoded image after releasing the image lock. Dispose of
  it inside the lock, with a regression check on image lifetime.
